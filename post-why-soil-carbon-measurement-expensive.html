The traditional method for measuring Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) is a major bottleneck for the carbon credit market. It's accurate, but it's also expensive, slow, and doesn't scale.

The Hidden Cost

Lab analysis costs between $30 and $50 per hectare. For a 10,000-hectare project, that's $300,000–500,000 for a single measurement. Carbon projects require annual or biennial verification, so these costs multiply quickly.

Why So Expensive?

  • Manual field sampling by trained technicians
  • Specialized lab equipment and procedures
  • Limited throughput — labs can only process so many samples
  • Travel and logistics for remote farms
